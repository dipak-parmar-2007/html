Who We Are

LV Laser Engraving is a local, family-owned laser engraving shop right here in Las Vegas. Customers from across the valley drive in for precise artistic work they cannot get anywhere else.

Founded and operated by Brian Lack, a Las Vegas resident since 2004 with a background in graphic design and web development, our shop brings meticulous artistic detail to every single project.
